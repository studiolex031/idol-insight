Idol Insight is a colourful, independent and fully offline overview of the 2026 Swedish Idol season. It brings together source-backed information about broadcasts, documented participants and published performances. Missing information is shown as a gap rather than replaced with a guess.

View verified broadcast status, participants and performances; distinguish facts from anecdotes and experiments; follow sources and data-status labels; try a clearly labelled fictional scenario lab; and save a local editorial note.

No account, permissions or internet connection are required. The app contains no ads, analytics or tracking. External source links open in the system browser.

Idol Insight is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by TV4, Idol or the format rights holders. No official logos, programme images or videos are republished.
